# Tide (the playlist: Ocean)

**Prompt.** For the Ocean themes: slow, wide and wet. Pad chords that breathe in and out like a swell, a
marimba rolling the chord slowly underneath (the films' 1-5-3-5, slowed right down), and only the first and
third notes of the melody on top, like light catching on the water, an octave higher the second time round.
Every two bars a wave gathers for a few seconds and falls back, moving across the stereo field. No drums. The
same I-IV-I-V | I-IV-V-I phrase as the films. About a minute, looping with no seam.

**Spec.** D major, 60 bpm, 16 bars (64 s). Pads with a two-bar swell (45% depth), a sine bass on each root,
marimba eighths, bright plucks for the melody. Surf: dark filtered noise, a 35% rise and a long fall, panned
across. Reverb 38%, a big room. Mastered to -21 LUFS.
