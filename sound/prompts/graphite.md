# Graphite (the playlist: Paper & Charcoal)

**Prompt.** The Paper & Charcoal themes are black ink on white paper, so the music is a single instrument and
nothing else: the studio's tune on a soft felt piano, in a small dry room, the way someone plays it for themselves
late at night. The same I-IV-I-V | I-IV-V-I phrase and music-box melody as the films. The left hand gives the
root low on each downbeat and breaks the chord gently upward; the right hand plays the whole melody the first
time, and the second time only every other note, with a quiet echo an octave above. Every few bars, the faint
sound of a pencil crossing the page. About a minute, looping with no seam.

**Spec.** F major, 72 bpm, 16 bars (about 53 s). Felt piano: six slightly stretched partials, the high ones
decaying first, brighter when played harder. Pencil: a 0.7 s breath of bright filtered noise every fourth bar.
Reverb 18%. Mastered to -21 LUFS, like Linen.

**Plays.** In the studio's playlist, after Linen; pick it on the Sound page.
