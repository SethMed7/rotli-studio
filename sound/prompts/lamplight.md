# Lamplight (the playlist: Midnight)

**Prompt.** For the Midnight themes: the quietest track, for reading late. Low, dark pad chords with a very
slow swell, a sub bass holding each root, and one bell note a bar from the melody (two, every other bar), as
if heard from another room. In the second half a high, soft pluck answers each bar. Underneath, a faint
breath of night air and a little dust. The same I-IV-I-V | I-IV-V-I phrase as the films. About seventy
seconds, looping with no seam.

**Spec.** A-flat major, 54 bpm, 16 bars (about 71 s). Pads voiced two octaves low with a 3 s attack and a
four-bar swell. Bells with inharmonic partials. Night air: very dark noise at a whisper. Reverb 45%, the
biggest room in the playlist. Mastered to -21 LUFS.

**Plays.** In the studio's playlist, last; pick it on the Sound page.
