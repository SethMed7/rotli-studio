# Canopy (the playlist: Grove)

**Prompt.** For the Grove themes: green, wooden, a little livelier. The whole melody on a marimba, first
alone over a soft pad; the second time a round bass, a shaker on the eighths and a soft kick join in; the
third time the kick steps out and a high kalimba-like counter-line answers the tune. Birds call in the gaps
between phrases. The same I-IV-I-V | I-IV-V-I phrase as the films. Just over a minute, looping with no seam.

**Spec.** G major, 88 bpm, 24 bars (about 65 s) in three passes of the phrase. Marimba: a round fundamental
and a fourth partial that is gone in 40 ms. Shaker: bright noise, offbeats louder. Birds: six short sine
glides with a warble, panned to the edges. Reverb 24%. Mastered to -21 LUFS.
